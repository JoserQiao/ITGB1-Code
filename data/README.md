# Data manifest

`figure4` and `figure5` contain analysis-ready tables used directly by the plotting scripts. They preserve the exact statistic, confidence interval, FDR, grouping or ranked-gene information displayed in the figures.

`figure6` contains the processed TPM expression matrices and clinical sample-matching tables needed to recompute the Figure 6/S6 coexpression panel from expression data. The three cohorts are GSE71340 (ovarian cancer), GSE240671 (breast cancer) and GSE212810 (bladder cancer).

No patient identifiers beyond public GEO/TCGA sample accessions are included.
